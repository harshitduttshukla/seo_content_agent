What landed cost actually includes

A landed cost is the total a buyer pays to receive an order in their own country: product price, shipping, duty, import tax and any broker fee. Most EU member states apply import VAT at the standard rate on the full landed value. Duty and tax are calculated per HS code at the item level rather than estimated across the basket [CLM-012], which matters once an order mixes categories with different rates.

Why estimates cause refunds

When a checkout estimates rather than calculates, the gap appears later. The quoted figure is the amount charged, with no invoice arriving after delivery [CLM-014] — the alternative is a support ticket and, often, a refund.

[NEEDS-CLAIM: support-ticket reduction figure] v2 · removed in v3

How Rodier handles it

Rodier sells to 28 markets from one storefront and quotes duty at checkout in each. The tax logic, the language and the ad spend share one market list [CLM-002], so they do not drift apart.
